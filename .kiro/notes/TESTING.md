# What the owner should try

## A. First build
1. Delete any old Retold, then install the new Retold.ipa with your sideloading app.
2. Open Retold. You should see 11 game cards. Red, Blue and Yellow say "Add your cartridge";
   the rest say "Coming later".
3. Tap Red (or Blue or Yellow). Files opens: pick your game file (.gb, .gbc or a .zip). The game
   should open in the bedroom, facing the TV.
4. Walk with the D-pad: to the stairs at the top right, down, then out of the door at the bottom.
   The screen should fade through the door and you should be in Pallet Town.
5. Stand below the sign next to the fence on the left, face it, press A. The words should appear
   letter by letter; press A to go on.
6. Hold B while walking: you should run.
7. Walk north out of town. Route 1 should carry on with no loading screen. (Oak doesn't stop you
   yet.)
8. Turn the phone sideways: the map should fill the screen, with the buttons on the sides.
9. Tap the pause button (top right), then Settings. Try Colours, Text speed and Walking speed.
10. Pause, then Back to games. Tap the game again: you should be back where you were.

Not in yet: Start menu, sound, battles, Oak's grass scene, and many people say nothing yet.
