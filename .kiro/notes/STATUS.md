# Status

"Done" means the owner saw it work on his phone. Everything below is built and checked on the
desk (desk tool pictures, compared with the real game on cBoy), not yet on a phone.

## Built, not on a phone yet (first build)
- Cartridge check by SHA-1 for all 11 games; .zip files are opened to find the cartridge.
- Red, Blue, Yellow overworld: all 248/249 maps load and run with no crash (desk sweep).
  Map edges join seamlessly, doors fade, stairs and mats warp, ledges hop, people wander within
  the original's limits and turn to face you, items and hidden-at-start people follow the
  original's starting table. Water and flowers animate.
- Text box: letters one by one at the chosen speed, arrow, paragraphs, scrolling. Signs and
  plain-text people talk (Red: 479 of the map texts show words; the rest need the game's own
  scripts rebuilt).
- Colours: Super Game Boy set per town (the original's choice), classic green, grey.
- Widescreen: the view fills the phone in portrait and landscape (or the original 20x18).
- Settings: text speed, hold to speed text, walking speed, hold B to run and its speed, colours,
  screen fill, sharp or smooth pixels, door fades, button size, see-through, vibration, keep touch
  buttons with a controller.
- Controllers (MFi, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch pads) and keyboards. Resume where you left off.
